# Image provenance

Generated on 2026-11-01 with Higgsfield, model GPT Image 2.5, as original illustrations for this project. Characters are fictional. No real pupil, photo or teacher material was used as input.

Shared style prompt for objects: "soft 3D clay render, matte surface, rounded shapes, warm side light from upper left, centered with generous margin, transparent background. No text."

`banner-plans` and `banner-iep` were added later the same day with the same model and style.

Felt textures were then calmed locally to low contrast. Everything was resized and converted to WebP. Check the provider's current licence terms before commercial distribution.
